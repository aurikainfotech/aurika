Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about working with us? Here are some quick answers to our most common client inquiries.

Do you provide custom software solutions? +
Yes! We specialize in custom web development, cloud integrations, and tailored software. We don't believe in a one-size-fits-all approach; everything we build is specifically designed to solve your unique business challenges.
How long does it take to develop a website or app? +
The timeline depends entirely on the project's complexity and requirements. A standard business website might take 2-4 weeks, while a complex custom web application could take 2-3 months. We provide a highly detailed timeline and roadmap during our discovery phase.
What technologies do you use? +
We utilize modern, scalable technologies to ensure your product is future-proof. Our stack heavily features React, Node.js, Express, Python, MongoDB, and AWS for robust cloud infrastructure and deployments.
Do you provide ongoing maintenance and support? +
Absolutely. Launching your product is just the beginning. We offer dedicated post-launch support and maintenance packages to ensure your software remains updated, secure, and fully functional as your business scales.
How can I hire you for a project? +
You can reach out to us directly via WhatsApp or Email from the Contact section below. We will set up a free initial consultation to discuss your specific requirements, timeline, and provide a transparent quote.
